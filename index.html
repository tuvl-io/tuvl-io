Why tuvl is different

People and rules make the final call.

AI is brilliant at reading, writing and judging. Rules and people decide what is allowed. So in a tuvl solution the jobs are split, and every hand-off between them is on the record.

01

The AI stays on script

Every agent works inside a contract: what it may read, what it may decide, how long it may take and how much it may spend. Anything outside it is declined before it starts.

02

Money and decisions need a person

Limits, approvals and sign-offs are part of the workflow. The system waits for the right person, then carries on, even days later.

03

Every action has a receipt

Each step is recorded: which agent, what it saw, which rule applied, who approved. Ask why something happened and you get the answer straight from the record.

04

The core is open source

The engine that enforces all of this is public on tuvl.org. Read it, audit it, build on it.

How we work

What you approve is what gets built.

Every solution starts as a written spec you review. The software is checked against it before it ships, so AI does the work and you stay in control.

Specify

We write down the process with you: who does what, what is allowed, and where a person decides.

Approve

You sign off the spec. Any change needs a new approval, so scope and cost stay predictable.

Build and verify

Workflows, tests and screens are built and checked against the approved spec before anything goes live.

Ship and run

Live on cloud.tuvl.io or on your servers, with a record of every action the system took and why.

Open source · tuvl engine

We gave away the core of our business.

Most companies guard the engine behind their products. Ours is public on tuvl.org under Apache 2.0: the same engine every tuvl solution runs on.

Why? Software that runs your business should be open to inspection. Your developers should be able to build on it. And you should always be free to leave. We want you to stay because you choose to.
